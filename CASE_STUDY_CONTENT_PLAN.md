# Copper AM Case Study Daily Content Plan

## Site Positioning

COPPER 3DP is a B2B RFQ website for industrial copper additive manufacturing. The site should attract engineers, buyers, and technical teams who need copper parts reviewed for manufacturability, quotation, post-processing, and acceptance.

The business is not a generic instant-quote platform. The best conversion path remains:

- Send CAD, drawings, quantity, material preference, lead time, and critical requirements.
- Review whether copper AM is the right route.
- Clarify high-risk requirements before quotation when needed.

## Current Inventory

The article library already covers the main engineering foundations:

- RFQ preparation and CAD package readiness.
- Copper LPBF design rules, tolerances, process limits, and cost drivers.
- Pure copper, CuCrZr, and CuCr1Zr material selection.
- Microchannel cold plates, liquid cooling plates, heat exchangers, and heat sinks.
- Semiconductor equipment, RF/vacuum hardware, busbars, induction coils, and conformal cooling.
- Powder removal, leak testing, CT inspection, finishing, and surface/interface risks.

The strongest published case direction is:

- SiC power module copper cold plate case study.

The next content gap is a repeatable case-study lane that turns the existing guides into application-specific RFQ stories. These should not rewrite the same guide pages. Each case article should make one application, one constraint, and one quote decision concrete.

## Daily Publishing Strategy

Use a two-lane content rhythm:

- Foundation refresh lane: update or publish broad engineering guides only when they support many future internal links.
- Case-study lane: publish one representative industrial scenario at a time, focused on the buyer's decision and RFQ package.

For long-term daily publishing, the case-study lane should dominate. It gives the site more search surfaces without creating thin duplicates, because each case can be tied to a real application, geometry constraint, inspection method, material route, and acceptance risk.

## Case Study Rules

Each case article should include:

- A representative application scenario, not a fake named customer.
- The starting requirement and why conventional manufacturing was insufficient.
- Why copper AM was reviewed, not assumed.
- Material route discussion: pure copper, CuCrZr, or CuCr1Zr where relevant.
- The design changes that made the part quotable and testable.
- Inspection, post-processing, and acceptance plan.
- RFQ checklist.
- Internal links to foundation pages and adjacent application pages.
- Two or three generated visuals using TITAN visual logic: evidence, explanation, and validation.

Avoid:

- Rewriting a guide page with a new title.
- Claiming guaranteed success.
- Publishing a case without RFQ value.
- Using generic factory imagery.

This keeps daily publishing useful without making the site look like repeated cold plate content.

## Internal Link Pattern

Every new case article should link to:

- One fixed service or application page: `/rfq/`, `/copper-cold-plates/`, `/copper-heat-sinks/`, or `/materials/`.
- One RFQ foundation page.
- One material selection page.
- One design or process selection page.
- One inspection or post-processing page.
- Two adjacent application articles.

This keeps the site structure clear for users and search engines: foundation pages explain the rules, case pages show how the rules apply.
