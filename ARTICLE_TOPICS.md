## Google-Friendly Quality Gate

Before any new article is published, it should pass these checks:

- The article answers one real engineering or purchasing decision, not a broad keyword.
- It includes fit / not-fit guidance, material or process limits, and what the buyer should send for quotation.
- It contains practical specifics: geometry constraints, surfaces, tolerances, inspection, cleaning, pressure/vacuum, electrical load, thermal load, or post-processing route.
- It links to 8-12 relevant internal pages when the article is a high-value guide, with anchors that match reader intent.
- It avoids generic claims such as "best", "perfect", "unlimited", "guaranteed", or "instant quote".
- It uses one topic-specific generated image or technical visual; no reused generic copper render unless uniquely appropriate.
- It ends with a quotation-oriented next step tied to CAD, drawings, quantity, material preference, operating limits, and acceptance requirements.
- It is not a doorway page, spun variant, or scaled content page created only to manipulate rankings. Large-scale keyword coverage must come from distinct engineering intent, not templated near-duplicates.

## Keyword Expansion System For Large-Scale Search Coverage

The site can generate broad search coverage without becoming low quality by using a controlled keyword matrix. The rule is simple: expand keywords aggressively during planning, but publish only pages that answer a distinct engineering or RFQ decision.

Primary keyword families:

| Family                    | Examples                                                                                        | Publish when the article can answer                                                                  |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Material                  | pure copper, CuCrZr, CuCr1Zr, GRCop, copper alloy                                               | Material choice changes conductivity, strength, heat treatment, inspection, or cost                  |
| Process                   | copper LPBF, green laser copper printing, infrared laser copper printing, AM vs CNC, AM+brazing | Process route changes feasibility, tolerances, density, surface finish, or assembly risk             |
| Thermal component         | cold plate, microchannel cold plate, heat exchanger, heat sink, heat spreader, cooling jacket   | Geometry, pressure drop, cleaning, leak testing, or thermal interface decisions differ               |
| Electrical component      | busbar, connector, power distribution block, electrode, induction coil, inductor                | Current density, contact resistance, insulation, cooling, or post-processing requirements differ     |
| RF / microwave component  | waveguide, cavity, resonator, microwave filter, RF housing, antenna part                        | Frequency, surface finish, plating, conductivity path, vacuum, or dimensional control differs        |
| Semiconductor equipment   | wafer cooling block, RF/vacuum manifold, lithography thermal part, chip packaging cooling       | Cleanliness, vacuum, leak testing, thermal stability, or precision interfaces define the RFQ         |
| Mold and tooling          | conformal cooling insert, mold hot spot insert, die casting insert, tooling thermal component   | Channel placement, cycle time, temperature control, insert material, or post-machining differs       |
| Fluid handling            | manifold, flow distributor, mixing chamber, nozzle, hydraulic/cooling manifold                  | Internal routing, powder removal, pressure, flow balance, leak testing, or cleaning differs          |
| Aerospace / propulsion    | regenerative cooling, injector plate, satellite thermal part, aerospace heat exchanger          | Qualification, high heat flux, fatigue, inspection, or material evidence changes the buying decision |
| RFQ / validation modifier | cost, tolerance, surface finish, leak test, CT scan, cleaning, heat treatment, quotation input  | The article reduces quote ambiguity or prevents a common failed RFQ                                  |

Keyword modifier rules:

- Combine one component term with one application field and one decision modifier, for example: `CuCrZr 3D printed busbar cooling channels`, `copper LPBF RF cavity surface finish`, or `3D printed copper manifold leak testing`.
- Do not publish a new page just because the phrase order changes. `Copper 3D printed cold plate` and `3D printed copper cold plate` should normally be one article unless the search intent is different.
- Use comparison terms only when the article includes a real decision table, such as `pure copper vs CuCrZr`, `copper AM vs CNC`, or `monolithic vs brazed`.
- Use case-study terms only when the article gives a concrete engineering scenario, constraints, failure mode, validation route, and RFQ inputs.
- Use "near me", "cheap", "best", "instant", or consumer-style modifiers nowhere. They do not match the business.

Indexable page rules:

- Pillar pages: broad but deep, such as material selection, copper LPBF design rules, or copper AM applications. These should be rare and heavily internally linked.
- Application pages: only create when the application can support unique requirements, visuals, RFQ inputs, and internal links. Avoid thin pages for every tag.
- Engineering guides: the default format for long-tail terms. They should answer one practical decision in depth.
- Case-style articles: use for high-intent niches where an anonymized scenario makes the problem concrete.
- Tag and archive pages: keep conservative. Do not rely on weak tag pages for large-scale keyword coverage unless they become real curated landing pages with original copy, crawlable structure, and clear user value.

Operational target:

- Build coverage by publishing one distinct engineering page at a time, not by mass-generating pages.
- Each new page should intentionally capture 1 primary keyword, 3-6 secondary keyword variants, and 6-10 internal anchor phrases.
- Every 10 new posts should create or strengthen at least one cluster hub through internal links, not necessarily by adding a new indexable page.
