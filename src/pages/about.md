COPPER 3DP is the copper additive manufacturing platform operated by **Suzhou Como Precision Materials Co., Ltd.**, also presented internationally as [Como Precision](https://www.szcomo.com/).

The platform is built for engineers and purchasing teams sourcing function-critical copper parts. Typical projects include cold plates, heat exchangers, heat sinks, busbars, induction coils, RF and vacuum hardware, semiconductor equipment parts, and tooling inserts with internal cooling.

## How Production Is Organized

Como Precision operates as an engineering-focused supply-chain integrator. We coordinate the manufacturing route with qualified copper LPBF, heat-treatment, CNC finishing, surface-treatment, inspection, and export partners according to the drawing and quotation scope.

This model is stated directly because the accepted component may require more than one process. A printed blank, machined sealing face, heat-treated CuCrZr condition, leak test, CT review, and export documentation may be handled by different qualified resources under one project route.

The formal quotation identifies the material, manufacturing scope, finishing, inspection, documentation, lead time, and commercial terms proposed for the specific project.

## Copper AM Capability Scope

COPPER 3DP does not claim to own the printing equipment illustrated on this website. Projects are screened against public market capabilities and the qualified production resources available for the specific quotation.

As a technical reference, current 532 nm green-laser LPBF platforms publicly cover build volumes from 160 x 160 x 200 mm through the 350 x 350 x 500 mm class, with 20-120 um layer ranges and pure-copper or copper-alloy process routes. These platform values are not unconditional finished-part guarantees and do not establish a supplier relationship by themselves.

Achievable walls, channels, tolerances, density, conductivity, surface finish, and lead time depend on the selected alloy, geometry, orientation, heat treatment, post-machining, and acceptance requirements. See the [copper 3D printing capabilities page](/capabilities/) for the full scope and limitations.

## What We Quote

- Commercially pure copper and copper-chromium-zirconium alloy routes, subject to project requirements and availability.
- LPBF build planning, support strategy, powder-removal access, and witness-coupon requirements.
- Heat treatment, CNC machining, polishing, cleaning, plating, and finished-interface requirements.
- Dimensional inspection, CT review, pressure or leak testing, flow checks, conductivity evidence, and documentation when specified.
- Prototype and low-volume production programs, with repeat quantities reviewed against the selected process route.

## What We Need From You

Send STEP, X_T, native CAD, or drawings plus quantity, material preference if known, target delivery, critical dimensions, operating conditions, finishing, and inspection requirements.

Simple projects can move directly to quotation. Function-critical projects may need focused clarification before the route is priced.
